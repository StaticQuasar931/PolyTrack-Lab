PolyTrack Lab privacy

Your times, profiles, replays, physics setups, favorites, and controls stay in this browser. They are never sent to Kodub, Firebase, Google Analytics, or a ranked service.

Published HTTPS pages use Google Analytics for basic page and device usage measurement under the title PolyTrack Lab. Google receives network information such as your IP address. Our integration strips query strings and fragments from the measured page address and does not send game events or saved game data.

Advertising storage, advertising personalization, Google signals, and analytics cookie storage are denied. Google may receive cookieless measurement requests. Analytics is skipped on local previews and browsers signaling Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Blocking Analytics does not affect the game.

Public weekly-track downloads come from Kodub. Clearing this site's browser data removes local saves. Original PolyTrack is by Kodub; this is an unofficial Lab.
