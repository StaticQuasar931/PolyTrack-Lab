# PolyTrack Lab privacy

Your times, profiles, car recordings, physics setups, favorites, and key bindings are saved in this browser. They are never submitted to Kodub, Firebase, Google Analytics, or a ranked service.

Published HTTPS pages load Google Analytics using the shared property G-QXB7CHLEDT with the page title PolyTrack Lab. Google receives basic page and device usage information and network information such as your IP address. The page URL sent by this integration excludes its query string and fragment. Advertising storage, advertising personalization, Google signals, and analytics cookie storage are denied. This integration does not send game events or saved game data. Google may collect cookieless measurement requests under its consent mode.

Analytics is skipped on localhost, HTTP previews, and browsers signaling Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Blocking Google Analytics does not affect play. Kodub's public weekly-track downloads remain enabled. Clearing this site's browser data removes local saves.

Original PolyTrack engine and assets are by Kodub. This unofficial Lab does not provide cloud accounts or paid game services.
